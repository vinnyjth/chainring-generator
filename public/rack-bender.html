Reading the sheets

  • End A on the deck loop is the rear end of the left side rail (or the rear-center joint on a closed loop). On a leg it is the eyelet end. The drawing puts the bike's front on the right.
  • Marks are tangent points. The tube starts curving at the start mark and is straight again at the end mark. Arc length is CLR × angle in radians and is already in the cut length.
  • Rotation is measured looking down the tube from end A toward the bender, clockwise positive, relative to the plane of the previous bend. A 0 means the same plane, 180 means the opposite way in the same plane. Check the sign on your first lipped deck; benders differ in which way they read it.
  • Springback is set on the bender, not on the marks. Thin-wall CrMo in a hand bender usually needs 2° to 4°.
  • Coped lengths are tip to tip. Flattened eyelet ends include the tab past the hole center, so cut, bend, flatten, then drill on the mark.
  • Lapped legs run straight under the side rail for the lap length and are brazed along the seam. Straight struts are coped to the rail and brazed at the joint.
